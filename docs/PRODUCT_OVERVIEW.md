# CryptoVista X Enterprise - Product Overview

**Tagline:** Market Intelligence. Portfolio Clarity. Enterprise Control.  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Target Market:** Digital asset research desks, simulated trading academies, wealth operations, and enterprise fintech organizations.

---

## 1. Value Proposition

CryptoVista X Enterprise bridges the gap between digital asset exploration and corporate business operations. Rather than forcing institutions to toggle between external market screeners, standalone simulated spreadsheets, and disconnected generic CRMs, CryptoVista integrates all operational verticals into a single multi-tenant workspace.

## 3. Strict Truthful Boundaries

1. **Non-Custodial:** No digital asset custody, private key handling, or wallet generation.
2. **Simulation Only:** All order book fills and account balances are virtual simulations.
3. **Reference Data:** External market prices are indicative benchmark feeds, not actionable broker quotes.
4. **Synthetic Demonstrations:** KYC demonstrations and corporate onboarding flows utilize synthetic demo dossiers.
